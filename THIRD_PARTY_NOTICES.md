# Third-party sources and rights notice

XunZi-PD combines derived research context from third-party scientific resources. This notice provides attribution; it does not replace the original providers' licenses, terms of use, citation policies, or data-access conditions.

## STRING

Protein interaction context is derived from the STRING physical protein-protein interaction network. STRING names, identifiers, scores, and source data remain subject to STRING's terms and licensing requirements.

- Source: https://string-db.org/
- Resource represented in the application: STRING v12 physical interaction context

## Ensembl

Human and mouse gene identifiers and mapping references were derived from Ensembl release 112. Ensembl identifiers and reference data remain subject to Ensembl/EMBL-EBI policies.

- Source: https://www.ensembl.org/
- Release represented in the mapping layer: Ensembl 112

## Reactome

Pathway membership and official pathway names are derived from Reactome. Official names are preserved rather than translated. Reactome content remains subject to Reactome's terms, license, and citation guidance.

- Source: https://reactome.org/

## Gene Ontology

Biological-process annotations and official GO identifiers/names are derived from the Gene Ontology resource. GO content remains subject to the Gene Ontology Consortium's license and attribution guidance.

- Source: https://geneontology.org/

## NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog

PD genetics context is derived from the NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog ontology-annotated association release. Study accessions, PubMed identifiers, variants, alleles, and association records remain attributable to the Catalog and the underlying studies.

- Source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/
- Trait selection recorded by the frozen bundle: `MONDO_0005180` (Parkinson disease), using the Catalog's own mapping
